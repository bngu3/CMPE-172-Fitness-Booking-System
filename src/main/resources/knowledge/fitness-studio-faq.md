# Fitness Studio Policies and FAQ

## Hours
The studio is open Monday through Friday from 6:00 AM to 9:00 PM and Saturday and Sunday from 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM. Sessions must begin during opening hours. Availability shown by the booking system is authoritative.

## Cancellation policy
Customers may cancel their own booked appointment before its scheduled start time. Once a session has started, it cannot be cancelled. A cancellation releases the availability slot for another customer. Contact the studio for help with exceptional circumstances.

## Booking and payment
Customers may book only open future sessions. A booking is not complete until the system confirms it. Prices are shown in the availability listing and are charged according to the selected service. Do not promise a booking until the application confirms it.

## Services
The studio offers personal training, yoga, spin, and nutrition consultation when those services are listed as available. Session duration and price depend on the selected service and are shown on each availability listing.

## Arrival and conduct
Please arrive ten minutes before your session, wear suitable workout clothing, and bring a water bottle. Tell the instructor about relevant injuries or limitations before the session begins. Shared equipment should be cleaned after use.

## FAQ
To find a class, search the availability calendar by service and date. To view a reservation, open My Appointments. The AI assistant can explain these written policies, but the live booking system is the source of truth for session times, current availability, and account-specific appointment status.
